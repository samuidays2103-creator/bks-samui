Regular Events

Weekly meetings of the business community

WED 10:00

Business Wednesday

Every Wednesday at 10:00 Cafe Plankton, Koh Samui

Our weekly networking meeting is the heart of BKS. Come meet fellow entrepreneurs, share your latest projects, find partners and clients. The format is informal — grab a coffee and start connecting. Everyone is welcome, whether you've been on the island for years or just arrived.

TUE 22:00

Evening Business Walk

Every Tuesday at 22:00 Lake near the basketball court Duration: up to 2 hours

An evening networking walk for those who can't make morning meetings or prefer to think while walking. We walk, talk, and think together. An informal format where ideas flow naturally — perfect for discussing projects and making connections in a relaxed atmosphere.

SUN morning

Jungle Calling! Hikes on Samui

Every Sunday Various trails on Koh Samui Free, families welcome

Weekly jungle hikes exploring Samui's trails and hidden spots. We hike through the jungle, light campfires, brew tea and grill sausages. A perfect way to combine networking with adventure — 260+ members and growing. Families with kids are always welcome!

Contact @Sergey_samui
