Our Mission

BKS was created to bring together Russian-speaking entrepreneurs on Koh Samui into a strong, supportive community. We believe that together we are stronger — sharing knowledge, resources, and connections helps everyone grow.

Whether you're running a restaurant, developing real estate, or offering professional services — BKS is your network on the island.

Club Rules

Please read before joining

💼

Business Only

The group is dedicated to business management and all aspects of entrepreneurship. Only current, relevant experience. No coaching or psychology.

🤝

Active Entrepreneurs

Only active entrepreneurs and business owners. We communicate on a first-name basis.

💬

Respectful Communication

No insults or aggression. Criticize ideas, not people. Personal matters — in private messages. Think before posting to 1000+ members.

🚫

No Advertising

No ads, sale announcements, currency exchange, referral links, or service offers. Only a brief initial business introduction is allowed.

📅

Meetings

Meeting organizers coordinate all details with the administration in advance.

⚖

Thai Law

Respect Thai law. Insulting the royal family is strictly prohibited.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join BKS? +

Join our Telegram group @samuibiz — read the rules, introduce yourself and your business. Membership is free.

Is there a membership fee? +

No, BKS membership is free. We are a community, not a commercial project.

Who can join? +

Active entrepreneurs and business owners on Koh Samui. The group is focused on real business experience.

Where and when are the meetings? +

Business Wednesday — every Wednesday at 10:00, Plankton cafe. Business Walk — Tuesday at 22:00. Jungle hikes — Sundays.

How do I add my business to the directory? +

Contact the administration in the Telegram group. Business directory placement is available for active club members.
